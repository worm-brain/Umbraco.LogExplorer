# Share a view

Everything you see in the explorer lives in the page's URL: the tab, the source, the time range,
any zoom, the filters, the level toggles, a native query, the sort order, the show-query panel and
Around this. A link, a bookmark, a reload or the browser's Back button all reopen exactly the same
view.

## Copy a link

Choose the **Copy link to this view** button at the right of the query bar, on the Search,
Patterns or Overview view. The explorer copies the link and confirms with "Link to this exact view
copied". Paste it into a ticket or a chat message.

Whoever opens it needs a backoffice account with access to the Settings section, and to the
source in the link.

## Relative and absolute ranges in links

- A **relative** range such as **Last 1 hour** stays relative: the link shows the last hour at the
  time it is opened.
- A **custom** range or a histogram **zoom** is absolute: the link shows the same minutes whenever
  it is opened.

To send a colleague the error you are looking at, zoom in on it or open it with **Around this**
before you copy the link.

## What a link does not carry

The fields panel's width and its open or collapsed state are preferences of your browser, not part
of the view.
